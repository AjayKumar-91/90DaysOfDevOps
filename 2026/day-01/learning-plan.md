# 90 Days DevOps Learning Plan

## Current Level

Experienced IT professional with 7+ years of experience including 4+ years in Java Development and Production
Support (L2). I have basic exposure to Linux, cloud concepts, and deployment workflows, but I am currently transitioning into DevOps and Cloud Engineering to build stronger infrastructure and automation skills.

---

## Why I am Learning DevOps & Cloud
I want to move from application-level development to end-to-end system ownership. DevOps and Cloud skills will help me:
- Understand how production systems are built, deployed, and maintained
- Improve my career opportunities in SRE / DevOps / Cloud Engineering roles
- Gain hands-on experience with scalable infrastructure and automation tools

My goal is to become a confident DevOps engineer who can design, deploy, and manage production-ready systems.

---

## 90-Day Goals
By the end of 90 days, I aim to achieve:

1. Build and deploy a production-ready application using CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions / Jenkins)
2. Gain hands-on experience with Kubernetes by deploying and managing containerized applications
3. Learn AWS fundamentals and deploy at least 2 cloud-based projects (EC2, S3, IAM, VPC basics)

---

## Core DevOps Skills to Build
1. Linux & Troubleshooting (process management, logs, networking basics)
2. CI/CD Pipelines (automation, build, test, deploy workflows)
3. Cloud & Containerization (AWS basics, Docker, Kubernetes fundamentals)

---

## Weekly Time Commitment
- Weekdays: 2–2.5 hours daily
- Weekends: 4–6 hours daily
- Total: ~18–20 hours per week

Consistency is my top priority over speed.

---

## Execution Strategy
- Practice daily hands-on labs instead of only watching tutorials
- Document every day’s learning in GitHub
- Build small projects weekly to reinforce concepts
- Focus on real-world problem solving, not theory overload

---

## Commitment
I will stay consistent for the next 90 days, track my progress daily, and focus on practical DevOps skills that prepare me for real production environments.
